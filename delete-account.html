Account · Data deletion

Delete your
account

You can delete your Roam account from inside the app at any time. No app any more? Email us and we will do it for you.

Two ways out

Either one ends with your account gone and your personal data deleted.

01 / IN THE APP

From settings

  1. Open Roam and go to Profile.
  2. Tap the gear next to your name to open Settings.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account, then tap it again to confirm.

TAKES EFFECT AT ONCE · YOU ARE SIGNED OUT

02 / WITHOUT THE APP

By email

Email us from the address you signed up with and say you want your account deleted. We will reply once it is done.

EMAILwhowantswhat.studio@gmail.com

DONE WITHIN 30 DAYS

What happens
to your data

Anything that is yours alone goes. Roads and reviews other riders rely on stay up, with your name taken off.

DELETED

Gone for good

  • Email, password, Google sign-in LOGIN
  • Your name and profile picture PROFILE
  • Every run, including its GPS track RUNS
  • Routes you saved SAVES
  • Crews you own, meets you host CREWS
  • Your place in other crews and meets MEMBERSHIP
KEPT, WITHOUT YOUR NAME

Stays on the road

  • Routes you posted ROUTES
  • Reviews you wrote REVIEWS

Other riders have driven these and rely on them. They show a Rider number instead of your name, and nothing links them back to you.

This cannot be undone

A deleted account cannot be brought back. You can sign up again with the same email, but you start from zero.
